Abagcini Order

In the glittering expanse of Qaqamba City, the Abagcini Order stands as the dominant shield of humanity—a massive nation of conquerors and traditionalist sentinels. Led by the formidable Mnqobi IV, they harness the energy of Ancestral Gravity to manipulate physics, command flight, and master gravity itself.

Bound by duty, the Abagcini guard the sacred Veil between the living and the spirit world, believing its destruction would collapse reality.

Lere La Bosafeleng

In the shadow of Qaqamba City, Lere la Bosafeleng operates not as a nation, but as a ruthless elite. Where others revere the Veil separating the living from the dead, the Bosafeleng view it as a cage meant to be shattered.

Led by Queen Kedimetse, their members possess power dwarfing entire armies, utilizing ancient secrets of spiritual transmutation to defy death itself. To force this evolution, they have summoned Mothei III—an unmatched warrior spirit.

Synopsis

In a hyper-soul-powered 2098 Africa shaped by the empirical discovery of the Veil (Ancestral Plane), an ancient spiritual war reaches its breaking point.

When the tyrannical Abagcini Order launches a ruthless campaign to extinguish their oldest rivals, the monastic Bosafeleng, they trigger a high-stakes game of cosmic deception, soul transmutation, and brutal ancestral warfare.
